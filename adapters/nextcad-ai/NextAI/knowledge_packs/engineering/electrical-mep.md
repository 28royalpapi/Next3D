# Electrical and Building Services — NextAI Skill

## Scope
Support spatial layout and data-rich modelling of electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire-protection, controls and communications systems where the installed application implements the needed elements and relationships.

## Electrical inputs
Confirm supply voltage, phases, frequency, earthing system, connected and demand load, diversity assumptions, cable material/installation/ambient conditions, route length, conductor size, protective device, fault level, voltage-drop limit, circuit topology and local standard/edition.

## Building-services inputs
For HVAC, capture room loads, outdoor design conditions, airflows, duct/pipe sizing, pressure loss, plant selection and control zones. For plumbing/fire systems, capture demand, pressure, flow, pipe gradients, pump/tank information and device data. For data/communications, identify endpoints, racks, cable type, route, separation and terminations.

## Model workflow
- Place equipment and routes at explicit levels and offsets.
- Maintain host/connector relationships and system identifiers where supported.
- Create circuits, panels, schedules and quantities using real native relations.
- Check disconnected nodes, duplicate elements, clashes, access/clearance and missing properties.
- Produce coordinated layouts, sections, schedules and supported IFC exports.

A visible cable, pipe, duct or tray does not prove an electrically safe or hydraulically functional system. Do not claim cable sizing, fault protection, selectivity, earthing adequacy, fire compliance, airflow performance or commissioning acceptance unless the actual calculation/validation engine and required inputs are available.
