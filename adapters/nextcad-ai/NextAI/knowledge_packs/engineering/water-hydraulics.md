# Water, Drainage and Hydraulics — NextAI Skill

## Scope
Assist with water supply, drainage, stormwater, wastewater, pipe and channel modelling where native tools exist. A drawn pipe is not proof of a hydraulically connected or adequate network.

## Required inputs
Clarify source and demand, population/occupancy basis, design horizon, flow units, peak factors, service pressure, levels, pipe material/diameter/roughness, route lengths, fittings, valves, tank/pump data, control logic, boundary conditions, invert levels, gradients and receiving-water constraints.

## Workflow
- Establish coordinate system, survey levels and units.
- Model network nodes, pipes, chambers, tanks, pumps, valves and fixtures as supported native elements.
- Preserve upstream/downstream direction, inverts, slopes, connectivity and system identifiers.
- Check connectivity, disconnected branches, impossible gradients and missing properties.
- Run hydraulic calculations or simulation only through a validated engine with traceable inputs and boundary conditions.
- Generate layout, profile, section, schedules and quantities from the native model.

Do not invent pipe sizing, design flows, headloss, pump duty, flood levels, water quality or statutory compliance. Cite the governing local standard/authority and edition if a compliance question is asked.
