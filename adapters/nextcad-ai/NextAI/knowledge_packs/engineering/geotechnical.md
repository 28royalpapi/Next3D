# Geotechnical Engineering — NextAI Skill

## Scope
Organise ground investigation information, geotechnical model geometry, foundation concepts and analysis inputs. Never invent site conditions or design soil parameters.

## Required evidence
Record borehole/trial-pit location and coordinates, ground level, strata boundaries, sample depth, field/lab test method, groundwater level/date, soil/rock description, derived parameters, units, characteristic/design values and source. Preserve uncertainty and variability across the site.

Relevant inputs may include unit weight, cohesion, friction angle, undrained shear strength, stiffness/modulus, permeability, compressibility, consolidation parameters, rock mass description, groundwater pressures, scour/erosion and seismic conditions. Each value must retain test method and source.

## Workflow
1. Import/record survey and investigation data without changing source values.
2. Build a stratigraphy and groundwater interpretation with confidence/uncertainty labels.
3. Define the proposed foundation/retaining/earthwork geometry and levels.
4. Identify service and ultimate limit states and construction stages.
5. Check bearing, settlement, sliding, overturning, uplift, global stability, seepage and earth pressure only with validated calculation methods and required inputs.
6. Report what is unknown and what requires a geotechnical professional's review.

A terrain surface or borehole symbol in CAD/BIM is not a ground model verified by investigation. Do not claim safe bearing pressure, settlement or slope stability without an auditable calculation and applicable site data.
