# Civil and Structural Engineering — NextAI Skill

## Scope
Prepare and coordinate civil/structural models, drawings and engineering information. Structural analysis and design checks require a validated calculation or solver workflow; BIM geometry is not proof of capacity.

## Inputs required
Identify project location and standards edition, structural system, grids and levels, support conditions, load path, spans, materials/strengths, member types and sections, connections, actions and combinations, serviceability limits, durability/fire exposure and construction stages. For concrete, capture reinforcement grade, cover, spacing, anchorage and detailing intent. For steel, capture section, grade, buckling lengths, restraints and connection assumptions.

## Model workflow
- Establish coordinate origin, grids, levels, units and survey references.
- Place foundations, columns, beams, braces, slabs, walls and reinforcement only with supported native element tools.
- Keep member type, material, offsets, level and orientation explicit.
- Record relationships between members and the load path.
- Coordinate openings, MEP penetrations and architectural constraints.
- Generate plans, framing plans, sections, elevations, details and schedules from the native project.
- Validate element count, dimensions, joins, connectivity, quantities, and project save/reopen.

## Analysis boundary
Do not invent loads, combinations, restraint assumptions, soil properties or code clauses. Do not claim that a beam, column, slab, retaining wall or foundation is safe merely because it exists in the model. Report FEM results only with solver version, element type, mesh quality, boundary conditions, load cases and convergence limitations.
