# Construction Delivery — NextAI Skill

## Scope
Help teams prepare coordinated model deliverables, quantities, construction documentation and issue tracking from the native model. Do not invent commercial or programme data.

## Required project data
Confirm project location, contract/package boundaries, work breakdown, programme/calendar, construction methods, temporary works, procurement lead times, resource assumptions, measurement rules, approved rates, revision status and information-exchange requirements.

## Workflow
1. Read model revision, coordinate systems, levels, phases and element identifiers.
2. Identify constructible work packages and relationships without changing design intent silently.
3. Generate quantities from native element properties and state the measurement rule.
4. Link schedule activities to model elements only where unique identifiers and sequencing logic exist.
5. Highlight access, temporary works, lifting, installation, clearance and sequencing questions for review.
6. Produce drawing registers, schedules, issue lists and approved exchange formats from the native project.
7. Record source revision, validation, approvals and unresolved issues.

Do not fabricate costs, rates, durations, productivity, carbon factors or material availability. Any estimate must state currency, location, price date, source and assumptions. A 4D/5D presentation does not make a schedule or cost plan contractually reliable without reviewed source data.
